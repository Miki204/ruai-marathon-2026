ABOUT THE EVENT

Run for the Elderly

The Ruai Marathon 2026 is a fundraising marathon organized by the Anglican Church of Kenya, Diocese of Nairobi.

The marathon brings together church members, runners, families, partners and friends of the community to support an important cause: building a home for our elderly.
